Pharmacology

High-Alert: Digoxin (Lanoxin)

Therapeutic Level: 0.5 - 2.0 ng/mL

Nursing Action: Check apical pulse for 1 full minute before administration.

Why? Digoxin is a negative chronotrope (slows heart rate). If HR < 60 in adults, withhold and notify provider. Watch for hypokalemia (potentiates toxicity).
Maternal Health

Fetal Heart Rate: Early Decelerations

Assessment: Symmetrical decrease and return to baseline associated with uterine contraction.

Mechanism: Fetal Head Compression.
Nursing Action: Generally benign. Document and continue to monitor. No immediate intervention required.
